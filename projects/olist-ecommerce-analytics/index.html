  • How are delivery delays related to review scores?
  • Which payment methods and installment patterns are common?

Checks before analysis

  • No duplicate order IDs in the profiled snapshot
  • No order items or payment rows without a matching order
  • No negative item prices or freight amounts
  • Category-level item totals reconcile to eligible-order GMV

How I avoided inflated totals

Order items, payments and reviews can contain multiple rows per order. Joining all of them directly can multiply records. I calculate item revenue at item grain and aggregate related tables before combining them at order level.
