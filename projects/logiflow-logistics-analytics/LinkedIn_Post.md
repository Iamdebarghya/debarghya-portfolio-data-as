# LinkedIn Project Post — LogiFlow

I’m sharing **LogiFlow**, a Power BI case study focused on last-mile delivery performance and operational decision-making.

Using a publicly available dataset of 25,000 delivery records, the analysis explores:
- On-time delivery performance across delivery modes
- Partner reliability, delivery cost and customer ratings
- Delay patterns by region, weather, vehicle and distance
- Data-quality issues that can change business conclusions
- A scenario-based estimate of potential delay-related cost

**Tools:** Power BI, DAX, SQL, Power Query and data modelling.

One finding documented by the original dataset project stood out: delivery mode appears to be a much stronger differentiator of on-time performance than region or delivery partner. I am treating those figures as source-reported until independently reproduced from the raw CSV.

This is an independent portfolio project using public data. The raw dataset remains credited to its original source, and the 15% delay-cost calculation is a scenario assumption, not an actual financial loss.
