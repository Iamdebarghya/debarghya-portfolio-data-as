### 1. Olist Brazilian E-commerce Analytics
- Business questions: order value and sales trends, category and state contribution, delivery performance and customer reviews.

## How to review
Start with the live portfolio, open a case study, then inspect its README, data source, metric definitions and source files. The [Recruiter & Technical Verification Guide](docs/VERIFICATION-GUIDE.md) explains how to reproduce selected projects and what limitations to keep in mind.

## Integrity and limitations
- Public and synthetic datasets are labelled as such; they are not presented as employer or client results.
- Metrics are accompanied by definitions and data-grain notes where relevant.
- The SQL warehouse uses synthetic seed records and is a modelling demonstration, not a hosted database.
- The HR dataset is fictional and must not be used to make decisions about real employees.
- Results in the CSV dashboards depend on the source files uploaded by the reviewer.
